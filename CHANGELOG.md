# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Read EPUB and PDF books one word at a time, at a fixed focal point.
- Chapters from the table of contents or PDF outline, with headings detected in the text.
- PDF headers, footers and page numbers removed, hyphenated words joined.
- Paused page view with dragging, a chapter list and optional context words.
- Settings for reading speed, text size and theme.
- Remembers your place in every book.
