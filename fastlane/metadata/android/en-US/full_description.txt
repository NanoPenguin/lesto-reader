Lesto is a minimal RSVP (Rapid Serial Visual Presentation) reader. It shows your books one word at a time at a fixed focal point, so your eyes stay still while you read.

<ul>
<li>Opens EPUB and PDF books from your device</li>
<li>Finds chapters, and skips headers, footers and page numbers in PDFs</li>
<li>Pause to see the page around the current word, and drag to move through it</li>
<li>Optional context words beside the current word</li>
<li>Adjustable reading speed, text size, and light or dark theme</li>
<li>Remembers where you left off in every book</li>
</ul>

No permissions requested, no network access, no ads and no tracking. Books are opened through the system file picker and never leave your device.
